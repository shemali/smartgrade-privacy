Privacy Policy — Markavi

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Markavi (package: com.smartgrade.app) is developed by Alshemali, an individual non-trader developer. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is used.

1. Data we collect

DataPurpose
Device ID (Android ID)Linking the subscription and free credits to the device, and preventing abuse of free credits after reinstalling
Purchase history (Google Play subscriptions)Verifying the subscription and unlocking paid features
Number of graded sheetsCounting the remaining free credits

This data is required for subscriptions to work. It is not used for advertising or tracking and is never sold.

2. Data we do not collect

  • Answer-sheet photos, student names and grades are processed and stored on your device only and are not sent to our servers.
  • The app has no accounts and does not collect your name, email, phone number or location.
  • If you manually enable the "external server" option in advanced settings, only the sheet photo is sent to the server address you enter yourself.

3. Service providers

  • Google Play: processes purchases and subscriptions. Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: subscription management provider that receives the device ID, purchase history and graded-sheet count on our behalf. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. Security

Data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS).

5. Retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. To request deletion of data linked to your device at RevenueCat, email us at the address below.

6. Children

The app is intended for teachers and is not directed at children.
